Privacy Policy

Updated October 26, 2026

TripVellum (旅序) is provided by 吴天宇, an individual developer, to help organize travel on this iPhone. Core features need no account or AI API key. Auto Scan is an optional feature you configure yourself.

Content stored on this iPhone

Trips, checklists, timeline entries, expenses, and source materials stay on this iPhone. TripVellum does not provide trip cloud sync. Inclusion in device backups depends on your iPhone backup settings.

Optional Auto Scan and DeepSeek

When configuring an API key, the app sends the key and a validation sample without personal content to DeepSeek. Consent is requested separately before the first transfer of travel material. Images and PDFs are read locally. Selected text, PDF page numbers, source date when available, current time, device time zone, language and region, trip dates, and default currency go directly to DeepSeek to organize structured itinerary entries. Original images, PDFs, and your existing timeline are not sent. If a source exceeds the text limit, only its beginning is sent and the app indicates that the source was not processed in full. Text may contain names, contact information, booking references, or other personal details and is not automatically redacted before sending. Choose only material you are authorized and willing to share. DeepSeek’s processing, retention, and billing follow its own policies and terms; TripVellum cannot delete data held by DeepSeek on your behalf.

API keys and network security

The API key is stored only in this iPhone’s local Keychain and does not sync. AI requests connect to fixed DeepSeek HTTPS endpoints. TripVellum does not host or relay requests and does not log keys, input bodies, or complete model responses.

Source materials and deletion

Source materials that add or update entries are retained locally. Original images, PDFs, text files, and pasted text remain complete even when only a prefix was scanned. PDFs record the pages processed; intermediate OCR text is not separately stored or indexed. Deleting a source removes its references while keeping entries. Unreferenced sources are removed after deleting their last linked entry, deleting a trip, or undoing an update. Timeline text and PDF sharing do not include source materials.

Permissions and analytics

Photos and Camera are used only when you choose an image source; the file picker reads only selected files. Manual features remain available after cancellation or denied permission. TripVellum uses no third-party behavioral analytics SDK or advertising tracking. System diagnostics follow your iPhone settings.

Independent exchange-rate requests

The ledger connects to Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) as needed to fetch reference exchange rates. Request parameters contain currencies and the requested date, without expense titles, participants, payment documents, or AI keys. A network connection also makes communication information such as an IP address available to the recipient. Frankfurter’s published privacy statement says it does not collect or log personal data, IP addresses, or request URLs and only views aggregate traffic statistics. Local bookkeeping remains available when rates cannot be fetched.

App Store purchases

Apple processes Full Access payments. The app uses verified transactions provided by Apple to determine access, without receiving payment card details or creating an app account or purchase server. Restoring purchases restores access; it does not upload, recover or synchronize local trips. Apple handles refund requests.

Your choices

You may leave AI unconfigured, remove your API key, withdraw recognition consent in Settings, or delete local sources and trips. Withdrawal affects future scans and cannot recall content already sent to DeepSeek. Requests concerning data held by DeepSeek should use the channels provided by that service.
