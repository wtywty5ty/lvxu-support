Help and Support

Updated October 18, 2026

旅序 helps organize travel checklists, itinerary entries, and expenses. These help topics are also available offline in the app.

What is included for free?

The Free Edition can retain one trip and use its checklist, timeline, ledger, and sharing features. Archived trips count toward the limit. Deleting a trip makes room for a new one.

What does Full Access include?

Full Access is a one-time purchase with no renewal. It removes the trip limit and includes all future paid features in 旅序. The App Store purchase confirmation displays the applicable price. Optional Auto Scan uses your own DeepSeek API key; provider fees are separate.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the Apple account that made the purchase and choose Restore Purchases from the purchase section in Settings. Restoring a purchase restores Full Access only; it does not recover or synchronize trip data.

Will trips sync to a new phone?

Trips and source materials stay on this iPhone. 旅序 does not provide cloud sync. Whether device backups include the data depends on system backup settings. Deleting a trip also deletes its checklist, entries, ledger, and source materials, so review anything you need to keep before confirming deletion.

Can I use the app without AI?

Yes. Checklists, manual itinerary entries, the ledger, and sharing need no API key. Auto Scan supports a standard metered DeepSeek API key you configure yourself. You can add entries manually if scanning fails. Review scan results; they do not represent live information from a carrier, hotel, or other service provider.

How can I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store purchases and refunds. Purchase Help in Settings provides a refund link.
